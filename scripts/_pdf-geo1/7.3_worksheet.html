7.3 Rectangles, Rhombi, and Squares
Rectangles, rhombi, and squares are all parallelograms, so they inherit every parallelogram property (opposite sides parallel and congruent, opposite angles congruent, consecutive angles supplementary, diagonals bisect each other) — plus one extra rule each. A rectangle has four right angles, which forces its diagonals to be congruent. A rhombus has four congruent sides, which forces its diagonals to be perpendicular and to bisect the vertex angles. A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus, so it has all of these properties at once. Because a rhombus's diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other, each half-diagonal pair and a side form a right triangle — the Pythagorean Theorem connects diagonal lengths to side lengths. This worksheet uses all of these relationships, often in combination.

Examples

Example 1: Rectangle diagonals are congruent
Rectangle ABCDABCDABCD has diagonal AC=16AC=16AC=16. Find BDBDBD, and find AOAOAO, BOBOBO, COCOCO, DODODO where OOO is the intersection of the diagonals.
Solution
A rectangle's diagonals are congruent and bisect each other.
Rectangle diagonals are always congruent: BD=AC=16BD=AC=16BD=AC=16.
Diagonals of any parallelogram bisect each other, so OOO is the midpoint of both diagonals: AO=OC=162=8AO=OC=\dfrac{16}{2}=8AO=OC=216​=8 and BO=OD=162=8BO=OD=\dfrac{16}{2}=8BO=OD=216​=8.
Answer: BD=16BD=16BD=16; all four segments AO=BO=CO=DO=8AO=BO=CO=DO=8AO=BO=CO=DO=8.
Example 2: Rhombus perimeter and diagonal
A rhombus has perimeter 404040. One diagonal is 121212. Find the side length and the other diagonal.
Solution
Diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular bisectors of each other, forming right triangles with the sides.
Side length: 404=10\dfrac{40}{4}=10440​=10.
The diagonals are perpendicular and bisect each other, so half of the given diagonal (666) and half of the unknown diagonal form the legs of a right triangle with the side (101010) as hypotenuse:
62+h2=102⇒36+h2=100⇒h2=64⇒h=86^2+h^2=10^2 \Rightarrow 36+h^2=100 \Rightarrow h^2=64 \Rightarrow h=862+h2=102⇒36+h2=100⇒h2=64⇒h=8.
Full diagonal =2(8)=16=2(8)=16=2(8)=16.
Answer: side =10=10=10; other diagonal =16=16=16.
Example 3: Rhombus side from both diagonals
A rhombus has diagonals 242424 and 101010. Find the side length.
Solution
The diagonals bisect each other at right angles, giving half-diagonals 121212 and 555.
Side =122+52=144+25=169=13=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\sqrt{144+25}=\sqrt{169}=13=122+52​=144+25​=169​=13.
Answer: 131313 (this is the 555-121212-131313 Pythagorean triple).
Example 4: Square diagonal and area
A square has side 555. Find the diagonal, and use it to find the area a second way.
Solution

Method 2 (diagonal): Area =d22=(52)22=502=25=\dfrac{d^2}{2}=\dfrac{(5\sqrt{2})^2}{2}=\dfrac{50}{2}=25=2d2​=2(52​)2​=250​=25. Same answer. ✓
Answer: diagonal =52=5\sqrt{2}=52​; area =25=25=25.

Example 6: Rhombus diagonals bisect the vertex angles
In rhombus ABCDABCDABCD, m∠A=70∘m\angle A=70^\circm∠A=70∘. Diagonal AC‾\overline{AC}AC is drawn. Find m∠BACm\angle BACm∠BAC and m∠DACm\angle DACm∠DAC, then find m∠Bm\angle Bm∠B.

Example 7: Square from its diagonal
A square has diagonal 10210\sqrt{2}102​. Find the side length and the perimeter.
Solution
Since d=s2d=s\sqrt{2}d=s2​: 102=s2⇒s=1010\sqrt{2}=s\sqrt{2} \Rightarrow s=10102​=s2​⇒s=10.
Perimeter =4(10)=40=4(10)=40=4(10)=40.
Answer: side =10=10=10; perimeter =40=40=40.
Example 8: Identify the figure from its properties
A parallelogram has perpendicular diagonals and also has a right angle. What figure must it be, and why?
Solution
Perpendicular diagonals in a parallelogram force all four sides to be congruent — that makes it a rhombus.
A rhombus with one right angle has all four angles equal to 90∘90^\circ90∘ (opposite angles congruent, consecutive angles supplementary) — that makes it a rectangle too.
A figure that is both a rhombus and a rectangle is a square.
Example 9: Rectangle diagonal from side lengths
A rectangle has sides 999 and 121212. Find the length of a diagonal.
Solution
A rectangle's diagonal is the hypotenuse of a right triangle formed with the two sides:
d=92+122=81+144=225=15d=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15d=92+122​=81+144​=225​=15.
Answer: 151515.
Example 10: Rhombus — side and one diagonal given, find the other diagonal and the area
A rhombus has side 171717 and one diagonal 161616. Find the other diagonal and the area of the rhombus.
Solution
Half of the known diagonal: 162=8\dfrac{16}{2}=8216​=8.
This is a leg of a right triangle with hypotenuse 171717 (the side): 82+h2=172⇒64+h2=289⇒h2=225⇒h=158^2+h^2=17^2 \Rightarrow 64+h^2=289 \Rightarrow h^2=225 \Rightarrow h=1582+h2=172⇒64+h2=289⇒h2=225⇒h=15.
Full other diagonal =2(15)=30=2(15)=30=2(15)=30.
Area of a rhombus =12d1d2=12(16)(30)=240=\dfrac{1}{2}d_1 d_2=\dfrac{1}{2}(16)(30)=240=21​d1​d2​=21​(16)(30)=240.
Answer: other diagonal =30=30=30; area =240=240=240.
