6.1 Pythagorean Theorem
In a right triangle with legs aaa and bbb and hypotenuse ccc, the Pythagorean Theorem says a2+b2=c2a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}a2+b2=c2. The converse lets you classify any triangle as acute, right, or obtuse by comparing a2+b2a^{2}+b^{2}a2+b2 with c2c^{2}c2 (where ccc is the longest side). This worksheet goes beyond one-step triples: expect radicals, multi-step applications, and classification with careful ordering of sides.

Examples

Example 1: Find the hypotenuse (full steps)
In right △ABC\triangle ABC△ABC with right angle at CCC, AC=9AC = 9AC=9 and BC=12BC = 12BC=12. Find ABABAB.
Solution
Right angle at CCC; hypotenuse is opposite the right angle.
Identify the hypotenuse: AB=cAB = cAB=c is opposite ∠C\angle C∠C.
Apply a2+b2=c2a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}a2+b2=c2:
92+122=c29^{2}+12^{2}=c^{2}92+122=c2
81+144=c281+144=c^{2}81+144=c2
c2=225c^{2}=225c2=225
c=225=15c=\sqrt{225}=15c=225​=15 (length is positive).
Answer: AB=15AB = 15AB=15.

Example 3: Converse — prove it is right
A triangle has sides 202020, 212121, and 292929. Show it is a right triangle.
Solution
Longest side is 292929, so test whether 202+212=29220^{2}+21^{2}=29^{2}202+212=292.
202+212=400+441=84120^{2}+21^{2}=400+441=841202+212=400+441=841

Equal ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ by the converse of the Pythagorean Theorem, the triangle is right (right angle opposite 292929).
