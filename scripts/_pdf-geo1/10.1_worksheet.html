10.1 Surface Area
Surface area (SA) is the total area of every face of a 3D solid — the amount of material needed to wrap it completely. Prisms and cylinders use SA=2B+PhSA=2B+PhSA=2B+Ph (or the equivalent 2(lw+lh+wh)2(lw+lh+wh)2(lw+lh+wh) for a box), pyramids and cones use a base plus lateral area built from the slant height ℓ\ellℓ (not the vertical height), and SA=4πr2SA=4\pi r^{2}SA=4πr2 for a sphere. Many real problems hide a Pythagorean-theorem step: the slant height of a cone or pyramid is rarely given outright — you often build it from the height and a base measurement first. This worksheet also covers composite solids (two shapes fused, with the shared seam excluded) and reverse problems where SA is known and a dimension is missing.

Examples

Example 1: Rectangular prism — full steps
Find the surface area of a rectangular prism with length 888, width 555, and height 666.
Solution
A box has 3 pairs of congruent faces: top/bottom, front/back, left/right.
A box has three pairs of congruent rectangular faces, so SA=2(lw+lh+wh)SA=2(lw+lh+wh)SA=2(lw+lh+wh).
lw=8(5)=40lw=8(5)=40lw=8(5)=40,   lh=8(6)=48lh=8(6)=48lh=8(6)=48,   wh=5(6)=30wh=5(6)=30wh=5(6)=30.
SA=2(40+48+30)=2(118)=236SA=2(40+48+30)=2(118)=236SA=2(40+48+30)=2(118)=236.
Answer: 236236236 square units.
Example 2: Cube
Find the surface area of a cube with edge length 777.
Solution
A cube has 666 congruent square faces, so SA=6s2SA=6s^{2}SA=6s2.
SA=6(72)=6(49)=294SA=6(7^{2})=6(49)=294SA=6(72)=6(49)=294.
Answer: 294294294 square units.
Example 3: Cylinder — full steps
Find the total surface area of a cylinder with radius 444 and height 999.
Solution
Total SA = two circular bases + the lateral (curved) surface.
Two circles plus the curved lateral surface: SA=2πr2+2πrh=2πr(r+h)SA=2\pi r^{2}+2\pi rh=2\pi r(r+h)SA=2πr2+2πrh=2πr(r+h).
SA=2π(4)(4+9)=2π(4)(13)SA=2\pi(4)(4+9)=2\pi(4)(13)SA=2π(4)(4+9)=2π(4)(13)
SA=104πSA=104\piSA=104π.
Answer: 104π104\pi104π square units (exact form — leave π\piπ in the answer).
Example 4: Square pyramid — slant height given
A square pyramid has base edge 101010 and slant height 131313. Find its total surface area.
Solution

Example 5: Square pyramid — find the slant height first
A square pyramid has base edge 101010 and (vertical) height 121212. Find its total surface area.
Solution
The slant height ℓ\ellℓ is not the height hhh — it runs from the apex down the middle of a triangular face to the midpoint of a base edge. It is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs hhh and half the base edge:
ℓ=h2+(s2)2=122+52=144+25=169=13\ell=\sqrt{h^{2}+\left(\dfrac{s}{2}\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{12^{2}+5^{2}}=\sqrt{144+25}=\sqrt{169}=13ℓ=h2+(2s​)2​=122+52​=144+25​=169​=13.
Now use ℓ=13\ell=13ℓ=13 exactly as in Example 4: B=100B=100B=100, P=40P=40P=40.
SA=100+12(40)(13)=100+260=360SA=100+\dfrac12(40)(13)=100+260=360SA=100+21​(40)(13)=100+260=360.
Answer: 360360360 square units. (Same numbers as Example 4 — this shows how h=12h=12h=12 and ℓ=13\ell=13ℓ=13 are connected.)
Example 6: Cone — find the slant height first
A cone has radius 555 and height 121212. Find its total surface area.
Solution
The slant height ℓ\ellℓ is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed by rrr and hhh.
Find ℓ\ellℓ using the Pythagorean Theorem: ℓ=r2+h2=25+144=169=13\ell=\sqrt{r^{2}+h^{2}}=\sqrt{25+144}=\sqrt{169}=13ℓ=r2+h2​=25+144​=169​=13.
SA=πr2+πrℓ=π(25)+π(5)(13)=25π+65πSA=\pi r^{2}+\pi r\ell=\pi(25)+\pi(5)(13)=25\pi+65\piSA=πr2+πrℓ=π(25)+π(5)(13)=25π+65π
SA=90πSA=90\piSA=90π.
Answer: 90π90\pi90π square units.
Example 7: Sphere
A sphere has diameter 181818. Find its surface area.
Solution
Remember: the sphere formula uses the radius, not the diameter.
Radius: r=182=9r=\dfrac{18}{2}=9r=218​=9.
SA=4πr2=4π(81)=324πSA=4\pi r^{2}=4\pi(81)=324\piSA=4πr2=4π(81)=324π.
Answer: 324π324\pi324π square units.
Example 8: Composite solid — silo (cylinder + hemisphere)
A grain silo is a cylinder of radius 666 and height 101010 topped by a hemispherical dome of the same radius. Find the total outer surface area (the silo sits on the ground, so ignore the bottom, and ignore the seam where the dome meets the cylinder).
Solution
+
Composite outer surface = cylinder's lateral area + hemisphere's curved area. No bottom circle, no flat seam circle (it's internal).
Step 1 — cylinder's lateral (curved) surface only (no top, no bottom, since the dome covers the top and the silo sits on the ground):
SAcyl, lateral=2πrh=2π(6)(10)=120πSA_{\text{cyl, lateral}}=2\pi rh=2\pi(6)(10)=120\piSAcyl, lateral​=2πrh=2π(6)(10)=120π.
Step 2 — hemisphere's curved surface only (half of a full sphere's SA, no flat circle since it's fused to the cylinder):
SAhemi=12(4πr2)=2πr2=2π(36)=72πSA_{\text{hemi}}=\dfrac12(4\pi r^{2})=2\pi r^{2}=2\pi(36)=72\piSAhemi​=21​(4πr2)=2πr2=2π(36)=72π.
Step 3 — add the pieces: SAtotal=120π+72π=192πSA_{\text{total}}=120\pi+72\pi=192\piSAtotal​=120π+72π=192π.
Answer: 192π192\pi192π square units.
Example 9: Lateral SA vs. total SA
A cylinder has radius 555 and height 888. (a) Find its lateral surface area. (b) Find its total surface area.
Solution
(a) Lateral SA is just the curved side — no circles: SAlat=2πrh=2π(5)(8)=80πSA_{\text{lat}}=2\pi rh=2\pi(5)(8)=80\piSAlat​=2πrh=2π(5)(8)=80π.
(b) Total SA adds both circular bases: SAtotal=2πrh+2πr2=80π+2π(25)=80π+50π=130πSA_{\text{total}}=2\pi rh+2\pi r^{2}=80\pi+2\pi(25)=80\pi+50\pi=130\piSAtotal​=2πrh+2πr2=80π+2π(25)=80π+50π=130π.
Answers: lateral =80π=80\pi=80π; total =130π=130\pi=130π. The difference, 50π50\pi50π, is exactly the area of the two circles.
Example 10: Reverse problem — solve for a missing dimension
A rectangular prism has length 101010, width 666, and total surface area 280280280. Find its height.
Solution
Set up the SA formula with hhh unknown:
SA=2(lw+lh+wh)=2(10(6)+10h+6h)=2(60+16h)=120+32hSA=2(lw+lh+wh)=2\big(10(6)+10h+6h\big)=2(60+16h)=120+32hSA=2(lw+lh+wh)=2(10(6)+10h+6h)=2(60+16h)=120+32h.
Set equal to the given SA: 120+32h=280120+32h=280120+32h=280
32h=16032h=16032h=160
h=5h=5h=5.
Check: 2(60+50+30)=2(140)=2802(60+50+30)=2(140)=2802(60+50+30)=2(140)=280. ✓
Answer: h=5h=5h=5.
Example 11: Open-top tank (no lid)
A cylindrical water tank with no lid has radius 333 and height 101010. Find the surface area of material needed to build it (walls + bottom only).
Solution
Only one circular base is needed (the bottom); the top is open:
SA=πr2+2πrh=π(9)+2π(3)(10)=9π+60πSA=\pi r^{2}+2\pi rh=\pi(9)+2\pi(3)(10)=9\pi+60\piSA=πr2+2πrh=π(9)+2π(3)(10)=9π+60π
SA=69πSA=69\piSA=69π.
Answer: 69π69\pi69π square units. Compare to a closed tank, which would need an extra 9π9\pi9π for the lid.
