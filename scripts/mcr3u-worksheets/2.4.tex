{\LARGE\bfseries\color{titleblue}Worksheet 2.4 --- Adding \& Subtracting Rational Expressions}\\[2pt]
{\small Grade 11 Functions, University (MCR3U) \textperiodcentered{} Unit 2: Equivalent Algebraic Expressions}
\end{center}

\begin{ideabox}
Fractions can only be added when they count equal-sized pieces, so we rewrite them over a \textbf{lowest common denominator (LCD)} found by \emph{factoring} the denominators. After combining, factor the numerator, cancel, and keep the restrictions from \emph{every} original denominator.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{LCD:} factor every denominator; use each different factor at its \emph{highest power}, counting shared factors once.
  \item \textbf{Build equivalent fractions:} multiply top and bottom by the factors a fraction is missing.
  \item \textbf{Subtracting:} bracket the whole second numerator --- the minus sign changes \emph{every} term inside.
  \item \textbf{Opposites:} $\dfrac{a}{x-4}$ and $\dfrac{b}{4-x}$ have a common denominator once you write $4-x=-(x-4)$.
  \item \textbf{Work rates and parallel circuits:} rates and reciprocals add: $\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y=\dfrac{x+y}{xy}$.
\end{itemize}
\end{ideabox}

\begin{learnbox}
\lh{The lowest common denominator}Factor every denominator. The LCD contains each different factor at its \textbf{highest power}; a factor shared by two denominators is counted \emph{once}. Multiply top and bottom of each fraction by what it is missing.
\lh{Subtracting safely}Put the whole second numerator in brackets: $\dfrac{a}{D}-\dfrac{b+c}{D}=\dfrac{a-(b+c)}{D}$. After combining, \textbf{factor the numerator} and cancel; keep the restrictions of every original denominator.
\lh{Opposites and compound fractions}$4-x=-(x-4)$ turns opposite denominators into equal ones. For a fraction inside a fraction, combine top and bottom separately, then multiply by the reciprocal.
\end{learnbox}

\sech{Worked Examples}

\begin{example}{ex0}{Example 1 --- Same denominator}
Simplify $\dfrac{9x+4}{2x-1}-\dfrac{3x-5}{2x-1}$.\soln $\dfrac{(9x+4)-(3x-5)}{2x-1}=\dfrac{9x+4-3x+5}{2x-1}$. \\ $=\dfrac{6x + 9}{(2x - 1)}=\dfrac{3(2x+3)}{2x-1}$, $x\ne\tfrac12$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex1}{Example 2 --- Monomial denominators}
Simplify $\dfrac{7}{10x}+\dfrac{3}{4x^3}$.\soln $10x=2\cdot5\cdot x$ and $4x^3=2^2\cdot x^3$, so LCD $=2^2\cdot5\cdot x^3=20x^3$. \\ $\dfrac{7\cdot2x^2}{20x^3}+\dfrac{3\cdot5}{20x^3}=\dfrac{14x^2 + 15}{20x^3}$, $x\ne0$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex2}{Example 3 --- Two binomial denominators}
Simplify $\dfrac{5}{x+1}+\dfrac{2}{x-4}$.\soln LCD $(x+1)(x-4)$. \\ $\dfrac{5(x-4)+2(x+1)}{(x+1)(x-4)}=\dfrac{5x-20+2x+2}{(x+1)(x-4)}=\dfrac{7x - 18}{(x + 1)(x - 4)}$, $x\ne-1,4$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex3}{Example 4 --- Subtracting}
Simplify $\dfrac{3}{x-6}-\dfrac{2}{x+1}$.\soln $\dfrac{3(x+1)-2(x-6)}{(x-6)(x+1)}$. Bracket the second product! \\ $3x+3-2x+12=x+15$: $\dfrac{x + 15}{(x - 6)(x + 1)}$, $x\ne6,-1$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex4}{Example 5 --- Factor the denominators first}
Simplify $\dfrac{4}{x^2-4}+\dfrac{1}{x^2-x-2}$.\soln $x^2-4=(x-2)(x+2)$; $x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)$. LCD $(x-2)(x+2)(x+1)$ --- $(x-2)$ is counted once. \\ Numerator: $4(x+1)+1(x+2)=5x+6$. \\ $\dfrac{5x + 6}{(x - 2)(x + 2)(x + 1)}$, $x\ne2,-2,-1$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex5}{Example 6 --- Opposite denominators}
Simplify $\dfrac{7}{x-5}-\dfrac{2}{5-x}$.\soln $\dfrac{2}{5-x}=-\dfrac{2}{x-5}$, so $\dfrac{7}{x-5}+\dfrac{2}{x-5}=\dfrac{9}{x-5}$, $x\ne5$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex6}{Example 7 --- Combine, then simplify}
Simplify $\dfrac{x}{x-5}-\dfrac{25}{x^2-5x}$.\soln $x^2-5x=x(x-5)$; LCD $x(x-5)$. \\ $\dfrac{x\cdot x-25}{x(x-5)}=\dfrac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x(x-5)}=\dfrac{x+5}{x}$, $x\ne0,5$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex7}{Example 8 --- Three terms}
Simplify $\dfrac2x+\dfrac{3}{x+2}-\dfrac{4}{x(x+2)}$.\soln LCD $x(x+2)$: $\dfrac{2(x+2)+3x-4}{x(x+2)}=\dfrac{5x}{x(x+2)}=\dfrac{5}{x+2}$, $x\ne0,-2$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex8}{Example 9 --- A compound fraction}
Simplify $\dfrac{x-\frac4x}{1-\frac2x}$.\soln Top: $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x}$. Bottom: $\dfrac{x-2}{x}$. Divide: $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x}\times\dfrac{x}{x-2}=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x-2}=x+2$, with $x\ne0,2$.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex0}{Example 10 --- Application: working together}
Alex mows a lawn in $x$ hours and Bea takes $(x+4)$ hours. Write the time together, and find it when Alex takes $6$ hours.\soln Rates add: $\dfrac1x+\dfrac1{x+4}=\dfrac{2x+4}{x(x+4)}$. Time $=\dfrac{x(x+4)}{2x+4}$. \\ For $x=6$: $\dfrac{60}{16}=3.75$ h. Check: $\tfrac16+\tfrac1{10}=\tfrac{16}{60}$ and $\tfrac{60}{16}=3.75$ \checkmark.
\end{example}

\begin{example}{ex1}{Example 11 --- Test the shortcut}
Is $\dfrac1x+\dfrac13=\dfrac{2}{x+3}$? Compare with $\dfrac{x+3}{3x}$.
\begin{center}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}\begin{tabular}{l|cccc}
$x$ & $1$ & $2$ & $3$ & $6$ \\ \hline
$\frac1x+\frac13$ & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $4/3$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $5/6$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $2/3$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $1/2$} \\
$\frac{2}{x+3}$ & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $1/2$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $2/5$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $1/3$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $2/9$} \\
$\frac{x+3}{3x}$ & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $4/3$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $5/6$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $2/3$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $1/2$} \\
\hline same? & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $\times$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $\times$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $\times$} & \makebox[1.35cm]{\strut $\times$} \\
\end{tabular}\end{center}
\soln The second row disagrees with the first at every value tested, so $\frac{2}{x+3}$ is \emph{not} equivalent: you never add denominators. The third row, built over the LCD $3x$, matches the first in every column.
\end{example}

\begin{keybox}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
  \item $\dfrac{(8x+1)-(2x+7)}{3x-2}=\dfrac{6x - 6}{(3x - 2)}=\dfrac{6(x-1)}{3x-2}$, $x\ne\tfrac23$.
  \item LCD $8x^2$: $\dfrac{5+3\cdot2x}{8x^2}=\dfrac{6x + 5}{8x^2}$, $x\ne0$.
  \item $\dfrac{6(x-3)+(x+2)}{(x+2)(x-3)}=\dfrac{7x - 16}{(x + 2)(x - 3)}$, $x\ne-2,3$.
  \item $\dfrac{2(x+4)-5(x-1)}{(x-1)(x+4)}=\dfrac{-3x + 13}{(x - 1)(x + 4)}$, $x\ne1,-4$.
  \item LCD $(x-4)(x+4)(x-3)$. Numerator $3(x-3)+2(x-4)=5x-17$. $\dfrac{5x - 17}{(x - 4)(x + 4)(x - 3)}$, $x\ne4,-4,3$.
  \item $\dfrac{x-1}{x-6}-\dfrac{x+8}{x-6}=\dfrac{-9}{x-6}$, $x\ne6$.
  \item LCD $x(x+3)$: $\dfrac{x^2-9}{x(x+3)}=\dfrac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x(x+3)}=\dfrac{x-3}{x}$, $x\ne0,-3$.
  \item LCD $(x-2)(x+2)$: $\dfrac{(x+2)-(x-2)-4}{(x-2)(x+2)}=\dfrac{0}{(x-2)(x+2)}=0$, $x\ne\pm2$.
  \item $\dfrac{x+3}{x}\times\dfrac{x^2}{(x-3)(x+3)}=\dfrac{x}{x-3}$, $x\ne0,3,-3$.
  \item $\dfrac1x+\dfrac1{x+6}=\dfrac{2x+6}{x(x+6)}$, so time $=\dfrac{x(x+6)}{2x+6}$. At $x=6$: $\dfrac{72}{18}=4$ hours.
  \item At $x=4$: $\tfrac24+\tfrac34=\tfrac54$ but $\tfrac{5}{8}$. Denominators are never added; use the LCD $4x$: $\dfrac2x+\dfrac34=\dfrac{8+3x}{4x}$.
  \item $x(x-1)$ and $(x-1)(x+1)$: LCD $x(x-1)(x+1)$. $\dfrac{(x+1)+x}{x(x-1)(x+1)}=\dfrac{2x + 1}{x(x - 1)(x + 1)}$, $x\ne0,1,-1$.
  \item $\dfrac1R=\dfrac{3+1}{3x}=\dfrac{4}{3x}$, so $R=\dfrac{3x}{4}$. For $x=8$: $6$ ohms.
  \item $\dfrac1n-\dfrac1{n+1}=\dfrac{(n+1)-n}{n(n+1)}=\dfrac1{n(n+1)}$. The sum is $(\tfrac12-\tfrac13)+(\tfrac13-\tfrac14)+\cdots+(\tfrac1{19}-\tfrac1{20})=\tfrac12-\tfrac1{20}=\tfrac{9}{20}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}
\end{keybox}
