# MCV4U — Calculus and Vectors, Grade 12 — curriculum alignment

Ontario curriculum (Grades 11–12 Mathematics, 2007), MCV4U strands:

- **A. Rate of Change** — A1 average vs instantaneous rate, secants/tangents, limits; A2 graphs of derivatives of polynomial, sinusoidal and exponential functions, the number *e*; A3 power, product, chain (and quotient) rules, problems.
- **B. Derivatives and Their Applications** — B1 connecting graphs and equations: increasing/decreasing, critical points, concavity, inflection, curve sketching (polynomial and simple rational); B2 problems: kinematics, rates of change, optimization.
- **C. Geometry and Algebra of Vectors** — C1 vectors geometrically and algebraically (2-D and 3-D); C2 operations: addition, scalar multiplication, dot and cross products, projections, applications; C3 equations of lines and planes; C4 intersections of lines and planes.
