# MTH1W — curriculum alignment (checked 2026-10-16; status updated 2026-10-17)

## Which curriculum applies

The link that was sent (`math910curr.pdf`) is the **2005** Grades 9–10 curriculum. Its page 47 is
actually *Grade 10 Academic (MPM2D)*; Grade 9 Academic (MPM1D) is on pages 30–38.

Ontario replaced MPM1D/MFM1P with the single de-streamed course **MTH1W (2021)**, which is
the course code used on this site. So the site follows the 2021 MTH1W expectations
(strands A–F, dcp.edu.gov.on.ca). Topics from the older Grade 9 Academic course that MTH1W
still needs (mixed numbers, order of operations with powers, fractional equations, rearranging
formulas) are taught in full. All lesson, worksheet, bank and test content is original.

Strand A (mathematical processes, SEL, culturally responsive connections) is woven through every
lesson rather than taught as its own chapter.
