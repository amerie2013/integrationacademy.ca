# MTH1W — curriculum and textbook alignment (checked 2026-10-16)

## Which curriculum applies

The link that was sent (`math910curr.pdf`) is the **2005** Grades 9–10 curriculum. Its page 47 is
actually *Grade 10 Academic (MPM2D)*; Grade 9 Academic (MPM1D) is on pages 30–38. The Nelson
*Principles of Mathematics 9* book was written for that 2005 MPM1D course.

Ontario replaced MPM1D/MFM1P with the single de-streamed course **MTH1W (2021)**, which is
the course code used on this site. So the site follows the 2021 MTH1W expectations
(strands A–F, dcp.edu.gov.on.ca), and uses the Nelson book as the depth benchmark. Where the
book teaches something MTH1W still needs (mixed numbers, order of operations with powers,
fractional equations, rearranging formulas), the site must teach it at least as well.

Strand A (mathematical processes, SEL, culturally responsive connections) is woven through every
lesson rather than taught as its own chapter.
