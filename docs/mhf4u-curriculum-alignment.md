# MHF4U — curriculum alignment and 8-chapter plan (2026-10-18)

## Which curriculum applies

MHF4U (Advanced Functions, Grade 12, University Preparation) follows *The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and
12: Mathematics* (2007), pages 85–96. Four strands, plus the seven mathematical process expectations:

- **A — Exponential and Logarithmic Functions**: A1 evaluating logarithms (log as the inverse of exponentiation,
  approximating logs, related exponential/log equations, laws of logarithms); A2 graphs of log functions (key
  features, inverse of the exponential, transformations y = a log(k(x − d)) + c, real-world scales: pH, Richter,
  decibels); A3 solving exponential equations (common base, logarithms), simple log equations, applications.
- **B — Trigonometric Functions**: B1 radian measure (arc length on the unit circle, exact values of special
  angles, reciprocal ratios); B2 graphs of sin, cos, tan and the reciprocal functions, sinusoidal functions
  y = a sin(k(x − d)) + c with radians, equations from graphs, models; B3 equivalent expressions, the compound
  angle formulas (exact values), proving identities, solving linear and quadratic trig equations on [0, 2π].
- **C — Polynomial and Rational Functions**: C1 polynomial functions (finite differences, degree and end
  behaviour, factored form and multiplicity, transformations, equations from conditions, families, even/odd);
  C2 rational functions (reciprocals of linear and quadratic functions, (ax + b)/(cx + d), key features,
  sketching); C3 remainder and factor theorems, factoring to degree 4, polynomial and simple rational equations,
  applications; C4 polynomial and simple rational inequalities (graphically and algebraically).
- **D — Characteristics of Functions**: D1 average and instantaneous rates of change (secants and tangents,
  shrinking intervals, applications); D2 combining functions (sum, difference, product, quotient, composition,
  inverses); D3 comparing functions and modelling, including problems solved graphically.

All lesson, worksheet, bank, assignment and test content is original. No textbook is named or cited.
